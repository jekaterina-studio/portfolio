biography

I am drawn to things that stand apart from familiar categories and do not necessarily follow dominant trends. I tend to look at situations from different perspectives, seeking out less obvious possibilities. When assessing a project, I recognise three subjective qualities: its ability to create beauty, the strength of its concept and the technical quality of its execution.

My path spans communication, visual arts and cultural project development, across creative and business contexts. I have worked on the development and coordination of different projects, taking care of research, content, organisation and production. This cross-disciplinary experience has brought me into contact with different fields, people and languages, often following an idea through to its realisation. Sometimes, a distinct professional identity is built precisely by moving across different disciplines and bringing their skills into relation.

In my work, I alternate between intuition and organisation, combining a symbolic way of thinking with a pragmatic one. I believe in expressing ideas clearly, listening to different points of view and addressing disagreements openly.
