Superconnection at Spazio Milesi

November - December 2024

part of Onda Studio communication

description

Superconnection was the first exhibition dedicated to the projects of Onda, an independent new media art studio based in Monza. Held at Spazio Milesi in Milan from 28 November to 14 December 2024, the exhibition unfolded over two levels and two exhibition spaces: an upper floor with four audiovisual and interactive installations, and a front wing dedicated to the installation Laser Echo.

The route explored the relationship between the individual, technology and the collective.
