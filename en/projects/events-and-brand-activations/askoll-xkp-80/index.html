Askoll XKP 80, local product launch

June 2024

part of GPone Milano - GPone Milano, Darsena, Milano

description

On 6 June 2024, GPone Milano organised the local launch of the new Askoll XKP 80 e-scooter, hosting a public presentation and test-ride event at the Askoll Store in Milan's Darsena area.

The initiative aimed to introduce the new model through a hands-on experience, bringing the product, audience and local area together. Alongside the presentation and test rides, the project included communication, promotional and prospective-customer engagement activities, developed in coordination with the Askoll team.

I took responsibility for the project as GPone Milano's marketing lead, overseeing its different stages: from defining the event approach to production, coordinating the people involved and managing promotional activities.

activities

  • definition of the event concept, objectives and format

  • project management and coordination of preparation and delivery

  • management of the relationship with Askoll and coordination of marketing and promotional activities with the brand, including the press conference, M4 campaign, lead generation, engagement of influencers and selected customers

  • management of suppliers, permits, set-up and event services

  • coordination of invitations, RSVPs, guests and reception

  • supervision of photo and video documentation

  • budget management and post-event reporting

role

Project Lead

results

  • approximately 80 attendees

  • 20 test rides

  • 1 vehicle sold during the event
