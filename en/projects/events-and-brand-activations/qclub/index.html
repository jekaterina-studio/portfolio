Live Events at QClub

May 2026 - Present

Client: QClub

description

QClub is a long-established venue in Milan's underground queer and techno scene, dedicated to electronic music, artistic experimentation and independent club culture.

My work there, which began in May 2026, focuses on lighting for events, DJ sets and live performances. I work from artists' technical specifications and cues, interpreting their visual requirements and adapting the lighting to the flow of each performance. Alongside the technical work, I liaise with organisers, art directors and performers and, when needed, contribute to venue operations and team development.

activities

  • lighting operation during events, DJ sets and live performances

  • reading and applying artists' technical specifications and cues

  • coordination with organisers, art directors and performers

  • operational support for venue activities

  • occasional support with photo shoots, ticketing and cloakroom

  • assisting with scouting and onboarding new team members

role

Live Lighting Coordinator
