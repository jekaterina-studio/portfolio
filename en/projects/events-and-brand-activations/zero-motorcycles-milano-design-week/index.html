GPone Milano for Zero Motorcycles MDW24

April 2024

description

For Milan Design Week 2024, GPone Milano, the official Zero Motorcycles dealership, partnered with the brand on an event dedicated to electric mobility. The initiative introduced the brand's range of electric motorcycles to the public at BarconeMilano, on the Naviglio Grande, offering test rides at the GPone Milano Darsena premises and a close-up look at zero-emission mobility. The test ride sessions, available throughout the Fuorisalone period, were accompanied by refreshments and branded gadgets.

activities

  • on-site presence and operational support during the event

  • management of communication and promotion through social media posts, sponsored campaigns, registration forms and website banners

  • coordination of the production of offline promotional materials (gift cards)

  • on-site welcome and briefing of influencers

role

Project Coordinator

results

  • 3 event days

  • 23 test rides

  • 5 leads
