Oltre lo sguardo

June 2024

a project by Aedo Sperimentare Culture at the Museo Poldi Pezzoli

description

Oltre lo sguardo is a project developed by Aedo Sperimentare Culture together with the Museo Poldi Pezzoli, focusing on access to artistic heritage for blind and partially sighted people. Launched alongside the exhibition Piero della Francesca. Il Polittico agostiniano riunito, the project explored inclusive ways of experiencing artworks through tactile and visual boards, easy-to-read texts, Braille transcriptions and audio descriptions in Italian and English.

role

Communication and Project Assistant

contribution

I supported the Aedo team during the first edition of the project, helping organise the cultural mediation activities alongside Stefania Rossi, the Museum's cultural projects lead, and the Gruppo Giovani Museo Poldi Pezzoli. I also supported institutional communications and documented the activities.
