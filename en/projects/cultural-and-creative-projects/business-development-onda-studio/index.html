Collaboration with Onda Studio

November 2024 - April 2026

client Onda Studio

description

Onda Studio is an independent new media art studio based in Monza, working internationally on the development and production of audiovisual projects, immersive installations and interactive experiences.

I contributed to the development of new project and business opportunities, helping to expand the studio's activities and build relationships with partners, institutions and prospective clients.

activities

  • research and identification of international project and festival opportunities

  • scouting of funding calls, funding programmes and participation opportunities

  • development and coordination of applications and project proposals

  • research and activation of contacts with partners, festivals and prospective clients

role

Communication, Partnerships and Business Development Coordinator

results

Successful applications:

  • FACES - Évora 2027 application ↗

  • Uppsala Light Art Festival, 2026 edition

  • Blink Cincinnati, 2026 edition

  • Spectra - Scotland's Festival of Light, Aberdeen, 2026 edition

  • Orografie Sonore project, supported by the Fondazione della Comunità di Monza e Brianza

Research and direct outreach helped expand the studio's international network and develop new collaboration opportunities, including meetings with Dubai Design Week and Louis Vuitton's Global Digital Store Brand Experience department.
