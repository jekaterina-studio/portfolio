AscoltaMi

March 2023

a project by Aedo Sperimentare Culture in collaboration with the Circuito delle Case Museo di Milano

description

AscoltaMi is a cultural and educational project developed by Aedo Sperimentare Culture in collaboration with the Circuito delle Case Museo di Milano. It takes the form of an innovative educational podcast format designed to support new residents and people whose first language is not Italian in learning Italian as a second language (L2).

The project is based on the Global Approach, a language-teaching method that encourages learning through creative, multisensory and immersive experiences. The podcast becomes a way of engaging with the language through real-life situations, places and everyday contexts: the museum, the city, its spaces and the relationships that shape them. It is an extended learning experience that accompanies participants before, during and after their visit, connecting language, cultural heritage and everyday life.

activities

  • support for the development of the project and educational format, in collaboration with the educational and museum professionals involved

  • editing and adaptation of audio scripts to make them easier to understand

  • participation in podcast production, including voice work for one episode

  • graphic development and layout of the educational toolkit

  • support for Edition 0, accompanying participants throughout the programme, gathering feedback and documenting the experience
