FAI narratives

March - May 2024

a project by Aedo Sperimentare Culture with FAI - Fondo Ambiente Italiano

description

FAI chose to experiment with podcasts and immersive multilingual audio guides to broaden the ways in which its heritage could be explored and appreciated. To this end, it commissioned Aedo Sperimentare Culture to produce and coordinate a series of audio pieces dedicated to some of its properties.

Each production is shaped by specific historical and landscape research, allowing the distinctive features of each place to be explored and brought to life. Through audio storytelling, the content becomes an accessible and engaging way of discovering historical, artistic and landscape heritage, designed to guide visitors and appeal to both Italian and international audiences.

I supported the Aedo team in the production of audio content dedicated to Parco di Villa Gregoriana in Tivoli, Castello e Parco di Masino in Caravino, and Palazzo Moroni in Bergamo.

activities

  • support for documentary research and the gathering of historical and cultural information about the sites

  • support for music research and the selection of sound references

  • assistance across the different production stages of the audio narratives

  • support for content development and the realisation of the productions
