OFFLab Educational

July 2023 - September 2024

a project by Aedo Sperimentare Culture

description

OFFLab © is an Aedo project dedicated to environmental sustainability, active citizenship and the practical exploration of the 17 Sustainable Development Goals of the 2030 Agenda.

It takes the form of a sustainable, modular urban green design structure, complemented by an educational component designed to encourage learning and awareness-raising. Through learning activities and experiences, the project aims to build knowledge, encourage participation and translate the principles of sustainability into concrete actions and practices that respect life, the environment and communities. The educational programme can be adapted to different social and geographical contexts, with a particular focus on schools.

activities

  • contribution to the design of the educational programme

  • research and development of learning content and teaching proposals on sustainability

  • development of educational activities and pathways for children

  • discussions with educators, project leads and teachers to explore learning needs and inform the design of the activities
