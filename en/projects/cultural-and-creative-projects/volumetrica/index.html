VOLUMETRICA

July 2025 · 19.5 × 4.1 × 4.1 m

a project conceived and developed by Onda Studio

description

VOLUMETRICA is an immersive laser-light and sound installation conceived and developed by Onda Studio, exploring the relationship between light, space, geometry and perception. Inspired by Wassily Kandinsky's research into point, line and plane, the work is based on mathematical and optical principles: laser beams, calibrated mirrors and a multichannel spatial sound composition generate three-dimensional volumes of light that do more than illuminate the space - they help construct it.

Through alignments, depth and dynamic sequences of light and sound, Volumetrica transforms emptiness into an architectural composition to be explored or contemplated. Its modular and scalable structure allows the installation to adapt to different spaces and exhibition contexts, transforming an environment into an immersive experience.
