Onda Studio communication

November 2024 - April 2026

Client: Onda Studio and AreaOdeon

description

Onda Studio, Kernel Festival and AreaOdeon share a common history, but each has a distinct activity and identity: an independent new media art studio, an international festival dedicated to digital arts, and a local non-profit cultural organisation.

Onda Studio engaged me to identify and develop a digital communication approach suited to the needs of all three organisations and their respective channels. The work involved defining distinct editorial strategies and languages that could highlight each organisation's specific character while maintaining a coherent overall direction. It also included organising archive materials, managing contact databases and migrating the newsletter platform.

activities

  • development of digital and editorial communication strategies for the three organisations

  • definition of editorial plans, content and tone of voice for the different channels

  • management of social channels and independent content production, coordinating supporting resources

  • research, selection and reorganisation of archive materials

  • review, cleaning and segmentation of contact databases

  • coordination of database migration to a new newsletter platform

role

Communication, Partnerships and Business Development Coordinator

results

  • Definition of a coordinated editorial direction, with distinct content and language for each organisation

  • reorganisation of historical databases, including contact segmentation and migration to the new newsletter platform
