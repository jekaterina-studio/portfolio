Silver Italia

October 2025 - September 2026

Client: Silver Italia and Silver Snc

description

Silver operates in the dental sector, providing innovative technologies and systems. The communication project covers Silver Italia, the commercial branch focused on product promotion, and Silver Snc, an established business specialising in the servicing and repair of dental equipment.

I contribute to the digital communications of both organisations, helping define a shared strategy and a consistent communication identity, with content and recurring features tailored to their respective activities and audiences. The work mainly involves close collaboration with the sales director, the newsletter manager and the in-house graphic designer, keeping language, content and materials aligned.

activities

  • development and updating of the digital communication strategy

  • editorial planning and definition of thematic content series for social channels

  • ideation, production and coordination of content

  • management and coordination of social channels

  • performance monitoring and analysis

  • coordination of audiovisual productions (product still-life photography and audiovisual campaigns): ongoing

role

Social Media Manager

results

  • 112 social media posts published between October 2025 and September 2026

  • greater visual and communication consistency between Silver Italia and Silver Snc

  • development of a shared communication direction across social channels, graphic materials and newsletters
