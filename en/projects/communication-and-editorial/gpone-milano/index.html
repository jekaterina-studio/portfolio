GPone Milano

April 2023 - October 2025

client GPone Milano

description

GPone Milano was a commercial business in the two-wheel mobility sector, operating across two main areas: retail, as an authorised dealer for several motorcycle brands, and servicing and repairs through a network of four locations in Milan.

My role developed over time, moving from social media management to responsibility for marketing and communication activities. I contributed to the development of the brand's presence, overseeing communication strategy, content production, promotional campaigns, commercial partnerships and the coordination of initiatives and events.

A significant part of the work was carried out in-house, from researching and developing content to producing photography, video footage, interviews, copy and edits, as well as managing publication. I also oversaw the application of the new visual identity across communication materials, in collaboration with the graphic designer.

activities

  • development of marketing and communication strategy

  • management and development of social channels

  • editorial planning and coordination of communication activities

  • ideation, production and editing of photographic and video content

  • creation and management of advertising and promotional campaigns

  • development and coordination of commercial partnerships

  • application of the new visual identity across communication materials

  • organisation and coordination of events and brand activation initiatives

  • organisation of team-building activities
