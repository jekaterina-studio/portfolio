Visual and narrative identity for OFFLab

July 2023 - September 2024

a project by Aedo Sperimentare Culture

description

OFFLab is an Aedo project focused on urban green design, environmental sustainability, active citizenship and biodiversity, with an educational design component aimed at schools. To support its development and communicate its aims and potential, a narrative and visual identity was needed to convey its complexity and make it accessible to different audiences.

activities

  • I developed the project's narrative and identity, translating its content and objectives into a visual and editorial communication system.

  • I produced and captured original photographic material to document the project and its development, and worked on graphic concepts and presentation materials.

  • I also contributed to preparing presentations for technical and institutional stakeholders and prospective funders, to support meetings focused on promoting and developing the project.

role

Communication and Project Assistant

results

The final editorial publication, deposited with the Milan Patent Office as supporting documentation for the building engineering project.
