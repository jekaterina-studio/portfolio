Special project for ArtsLife

May - August 2024

Client: ArtsLife - We Web Company

description

ArtsLife is a long-established Italian online magazine specialising in the art market and a point of reference in art advisory. The magazine brought me in to work on the artist Andrei Esionov in the lead-up to his exhibition Strangers, presented in Venice in September 2024.

The initial brief involved developing an eight-month social communication strategy, designed to build an audience ahead of the exhibition through a narrative about the artist and his work. My actual contribution focused on the initial phase of the plan, developed over four months.

I was subsequently given the opportunity to prepare a strategic and editorial proposal for the development of ArtsLife's digital presence, which was not ultimately implemented.

activities

  • development of the social communication strategy and definition of narrative and editorial directions

  • ideation, planning and management of Instagram content

  • planning of advertising and promotional activities

  • preparation of a strategic and editorial proposal for ArtsLife's digital presence

role

Social Media and Communication Strategist
