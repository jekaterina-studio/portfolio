Aedo Sperimentare Culture

September 2022 - September 2024

organisation: Aedo Sperimentare Culture

description

Aedo Sperimentare Culture is a cultural organisation specialising in innovative cultural and educational projects, with a strong social and artistic focus.

During my time working with the organisation, I contributed to the renewal of its corporate communication, overseeing the application of the new visual identity and narrative system across its channels and materials. The work involved the website, social media, institutional presentations and communication tools, with the aim of building a recognisable image and a language consistent with Aedo's activities and projects.

activities

  • application and development of the new visual identity system across company channels and materials

  • retrieval and organisation of archive materials for communication

  • contribution to the content and structure of the new website

  • editorial planning and development of content strategies for social channels

  • ideation, production and publication of digital content

  • creation and updating of institutional presentations and stakeholder materials

  • coordination with the people involved in communication and graphic-material development

role

Communication and Project Assistant

results

  • Creation and launch of the new website

  • renewal of communication across social channels

  • application of the new visual identity to the organisation's main communication tools and materials
