floral design

description

Floral design has become a space for research in which thought, material and hands-on practice come together. Through botanical compositions, I explore shapes, volumes, textures and combinations, seeking a visual language capable of translating personal concepts, states of mind and symbolic meanings.

I am interested in the dialogue between natural and artificial materials: botanical elements placed alongside glass, ceramics, resins, metals and fabrics. Each composition emerges from a process of observation, study and design, in which intuition and technical precision work together to shape the form.

For me, technique and knowledge are inseparable dimensions: understanding a material means recognising its possibilities, while technical mastery makes it possible to transform them into expression.
