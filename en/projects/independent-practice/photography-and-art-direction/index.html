photography and art direction

description

Photography has always been a language through which I observe, interpret and build my relationship with the world. I am particularly interested in what lies beneath appearances: details, identities, vulnerability and aspects of people that do not always have the opportunity to emerge.

My practice is mainly developed through constructed images, carefully considered compositions and a precise approach to art direction. When working with other people, the process begins with encounter, listening and rapport: through conversation, trust and the shared construction of the scene, I try to bring out something that belongs to the subject, but that they may not yet have recognised in themselves.

The final image is the result of a planned vision, yet remains open to the emotions and interpretations of those who encounter it.
