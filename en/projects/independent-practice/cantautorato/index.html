songwriting

description

Writing is one of the ways in which I work through what I feel. Songs emerge from moods, personal experiences, periods of transition that shape my days, and observations of everyday life.

Intuitions, personal states and, finally, words gradually take on a rhythmic structure through a process of composition and experimentation with the acoustic guitar.

This is not a practice driven by answers, conclusions or success. I am simply interested in giving voice to what I feel the need to express through writing, and allowing it, naturally and without forcing it, to meet someone else's sensibility.
