practice

A project does not always coincide with what it claims it wants to achieve. Before identifying a direction, I seek to understand the intentions, expectations and motivations that shape it, including those that do not immediately fit into a brief or a measurable objective.

I am interested in gaining an in-depth understanding of what I help develop: the product, the context, the people and the relationships that define it. This understanding does not end with the initial research; it takes shape over time, through discussion and the work itself.

My approach combines conceptual attention with practicality. I look for the meaning that makes a project distinctive, without losing sight of the conditions that allow it to exist, evolve and find its place.
